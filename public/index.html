About the Cabin

Cosy studio cabin in the centre of Portsea. Perfect base to explore the peninsula or for weddings and functions. Short walk to Portsea front and back beach. Sorrento is a 5-minute drive away.


The space: Studio-style cabin with own entrance and driveway. Queen bed, large bathroom with separate shower and bath, kitchenette with fridge, split-system air conditioning/heating, lounge suites, Smart LG 50" TV with Netflix, free Wi-Fi, all linen and towels supplied.

Pets (especially dogs) very welcome. Secure yard when gates are closed. Please mention pet details in your request.
