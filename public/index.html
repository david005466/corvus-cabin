WELCOME TO CORVUS CABIN

A little hideaway.
A lot to love.

Tucked into the leafy heart of Portsea, Corvus Cabin is an easy-going place to retreat, reconnect and settle into peninsula time. Wander to the front or back beach, discover the neighbouring village of Sorrento, then return to your own cosy corner of the coast.

With a queen bed, kitchenette, generous bathroom and secure yard when the gates are closed, it’s a comfortable getaway for you and your four-legged companion.
